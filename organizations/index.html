Why a coach

Learning a new skill at work is disorienting: too many tools, too much advice, and no way to know if you're doing it right. A coach fixes both halves of that.

  • Clarity: a practitioner who's already through the noise shows each person what's worth learning and what to skip.

  • Accountability: someone is reading their work every week, which is the difference between a course that gets abandoned and a skill that gets built.
