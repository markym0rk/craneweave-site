Why a coach

AI skills grow through practice, feedback, and the confidence to apply them to real work. A coach brings all three together.

  • Clarity

    A practitioner helps each person focus on the tools and techniques that matter for their role.

  • Accountability

    Their coach reviews the work on an agreed cadence, turning one-time training into a skill that keeps developing.

  • Visibility

    Leaders can see participation, coaching engagement, and progress against the measures agreed before the pilot begins.

How it works

Baseline. Build. Review.

01

Baseline

Every participant starts with an assessment: what they use, their job, and their current access. Your report starts here too.

02

Build the foundations

Participants build the core skills that matter for their roles through real projects. Each skill becomes practical because they have already used it in their own work.

03

Get expert review

Participants submit their real work on the cadence defined for the pilot. An expert coach tells them what to change and why.

Know what the pilot includes before it starts.

Your written scope sets out:

  • Who participates

    Team size, roles, starting access, and the use cases the pilot will support.

  • How coaching runs

    Program length, practice cadence, review volume, coach matching, and coordinator support.

  • How progress is measured

    The baseline, success measures, and reporting view agreed before launch.

  • What work can be submitted

    Approved tools, confidentiality boundaries, and any organization-specific restrictions.

  • What it costs and when it starts

    The complete pilot price, timing, and included services in one written scope.

Review the scope and price before you commit. Nothing is charged until you approve them in writing. No sales call unless you want one.
