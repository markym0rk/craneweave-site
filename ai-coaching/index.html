How individual AI coaching works

Baseline. Build. Review.

01

Baseline

Start with your role, current tools, access, and the work you want to improve. Your coach turns that starting point into a practical plan.

02

Build

Develop the core skills through work you already need to do. Each project becomes useful immediately.

03

Review

Send the workflow, prompt, output, and relevant context. Your coach explains what to change, why it matters, and how to apply the lesson next time.

What gets reviewed

Build judgment you can use again.

Each review credit included in your plan covers one focused piece of work or workflow. Your coach can help you improve:

  • ✓Use case

    The use case

    Decide where AI can make a meaningful contribution and where your own judgment needs to stay in control.

  • ✓Instructions

    The instructions and context

    Give the tool a clear audience, purpose, constraints, and examples it can act on.

  • ✓Output

    The output

    Evaluate whether the result serves the task, then identify what needs to change in the setup or the next pass.

  • ✓Workflow

    The workflow

    Turn a promising one-off result into clear steps you can repeat, adapt, and improve.
