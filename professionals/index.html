The coaching system

One coaching system. Sized to you.

AI skills grow through practice, feedback, and the confidence to apply them to real work. Craneweave brings all three together.

01

Baseline

Start with each person’s role, current tools, access, and the work they want to improve.

02

Build

Develop the core skills through real projects rather than generic exercises.

03

Review

Send real work to a named coach and receive clear written feedback on what to change and why.

04

Progress

Apply the feedback to the next attempt. Individuals build repeatable workflows; team leaders receive a shareable view of participation and progress against the measures defined for the pilot.

Your submissions are for coaching—not model training.

Submitted work is used only to coach the person who submitted it. It is never sold or used to train AI models and is deleted within 90 days after coaching ends—sooner on request. In team programs, access is limited to the assigned coach and the Craneweave program coordinator.
