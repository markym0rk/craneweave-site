Questions about the college cycle.

When should you hire a college admissions coach?

The best time to hire a college admissions coach is junior spring — earlier is calmer, not more expensive; the price is the same in April as in October. Start then and the school list and personal statement exist before senior year. Fall works too: the plan compresses, and Plus and the 24-hour rush exist for exactly that.

What does the college cycle look like, month by month?

Month by month, the college cycle runs: junior spring — school list and testing plan; summer — the personal statement, drafted while there's time to throw one away; August to November — supplements school by school, the early rounds, and the University of California application in late November; December to January — regular deadlines, aid forms, and the last read before submit.

What does a coach read across a college cycle?

Across a college cycle, your coach reads four things: the school list — reach, target, and likely, calibrated to this student, not a ranking; the personal statement, drafts round after round; the supplements school by school, where most credits go from September to November; and the whole application before submit — every field nobody proofreads.

What does one review credit actually buy?

One review credit buys one scoped review of real work — an essay draft, a school list, a full application (Common App, UC, Coalition, or a school's own portal), or a check-in when something changes — returned in writing on a published turnaround and signed by your coach. Your coach helps you decide where each month's credits do the most good.

How much does a college admissions coach cost?

A college admissions coach costs $299 a month at Craneweave — Core: four review credits, 72-hour turnaround. Plus is $499: eight credits, 48 hours, priority matching. Seniors: the $1,999 Season Pass, August 1 to January 15, 24 credits. Credits reset each billing month; a 24-hour rush adds $50; cancel monthly, no contracts, no sales call. Private consultants charge $10,000+ per cycle.

Do you write the essays?

No — we don't write the essays. The student writes; the coach reads, judges, and says what to do next. The platform does the groundwork — pulling the file together, prepping the draft so the review starts fast — and a named coach applies the judgment and signs it. Nothing goes out under a student's name that they didn't write.

Which coach will we get?

The coach you get is matched to college admissions, has been through this process, and is named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards. Re-match any time, or ask for a second opinion — another vetted coach's read — for one credit. Parents and students can both message us; either can ask us to stop.

Do you guarantee admission?

No — we don't guarantee admission, and you should walk away from anyone who does. Admissions is not a purchasable outcome. What we promise is the work: every essay, list, and application read by a named coach on a published turnaround, early enough to act on it.
