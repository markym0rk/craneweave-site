How it works

Four steps. Then it’s just the work.

1

Apply

Name, school, admissions history. Five minutes. We read every application ourselves.

2

Get verified

We confirm your admissions history and calibrate on sample reviews. Your name and school go on every review you approve — verification is the product.

3

Declare capacity

Tell us how many reviews a week you want. Change it any time. Zero is a valid number during finals.

4

Review from your queue

Each review shows its scope and rate before you accept. AI has already done the busywork — you do the judging.

Pay

Transparent per-review rates.

Every review in your queue shows exactly what it pays before you accept it — scoped by type and length, not by how long you agonize. Payouts land biweekly. No invoicing, no chasing clients, no unpaid “discovery calls.” The overhead that eats tutoring gigs doesn’t exist here: students arrive prepared, gated by the program, with the busywork already done.
