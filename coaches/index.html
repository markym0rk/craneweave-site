Coaches don't list themselves here.

They apply, they're vetted against our review standards, and they stay only if their reviews hold that bar.

Who can coach here

You've been through the application you'd be reviewing, and you can show us where you landed. For college admissions that means an undergraduate program we can verify; for BS/MD, the double application itself. Past that: you write specifically, and you answer on time.

How we vet

We read every application ourselves. If it's a fit, we verify your program and your admissions history, then send you sample work to review, scored against the same standard student reviews are held to. That calibration step is the gate.

What every review has to do

Say something specific: what to cut, what's missing, what to do next. Land before the turnaround published on the review. Carry your name. Generic praise and line edits with no reasoning are how a review fails here, and a failed review gets re-reviewed at our cost, not the student's.

What you commit to

Nothing standing. You set your capacity one review at a time, and a week where you take none is a normal week. Routing is the only lever that moves: reviews go first to coaches whose work consistently holds the bar.

What we ask you not to do

Don't write the student's essay; coach it. Don't promise anyone an outcome. Don't take a family off-platform, because the consent, the protections, and the record of the student's file live here. If you have a personal stake in an applicant, flag it and we'll route the review elsewhere.

Questions coaches ask.

What does Craneweave pay per review?

Craneweave pays published per-review rates: $45 for an essay review, $30 for a school list, $110 for a full application, $20 for a check-in, and $25 on top for a 24-hour rush. The rates are the same for every coach, and you see the rate before you accept.

How do you become a college admissions coach here?

You become an admissions coach here by applying with what you've done: your school or program, your admissions history, your background. We read every application, verify it, and score a sample review against our standards. Once you're on, reviews arrive in a queue; you accept only what fits your expertise. No calendars, no unpaid intro calls.

How much will I make?

How much you make is your call: we publish what each review pays and nothing beyond that, because you decide how many you accept. Anyone quoting a monthly figure for work like this is guessing at your calendar. The rates are above; the arithmetic is yours.

Is there a minimum, or a schedule?

There's no minimum and no schedule — no shift, no quota, and no consequence for a quiet week. Reviews sit in your queue with a deadline attached and you take the ones you want. If you take none, the queue routes elsewhere until you're back.

Do I have to talk to students or parents?

You don't have to talk to students or parents; the work is written. A coordinator handles the relationship, the reminders, and anything administrative, so your side is the review itself. You're never asked to run an unpaid intro call or sell anything to a family.

What does the platform draft, and what's left for me?

The platform drafts the groundwork — what changed since the last draft, which prompt this answers, what comes next — and none of it goes out unread. The judgment is yours: what's working, what to cut, what's missing, what to do about it. Matching, reminders, and billing all happen before the file reaches you, so families pay expert prices only for your expert moments — that's how this costs $299, not $10,000.

Which goals can I coach?

You can coach the goals you've been through: college admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now, so that's where reviews are today. MBA, law school, medical school, and banking and consulting recruiting open next as founding cohorts. Tell us on the application which you've actually done — we route by experience, not interest.

Am I expected to guarantee results?

You're never asked to guarantee results. Admission isn't a purchasable outcome and we don't sell one — walk away from any platform that asks you to imply otherwise. What we promise families is the work: every essay, list, and application read by a named coach before it ships, on schedule. That's the only promise you keep.

What happens to a student's work and data?

A student's work and data are used only to coach that student, deleted within 90 days — sooner on request, and never sold. Parents consent before any student under 18 joins. That also means the work isn't yours to keep, reuse, or share once the review is returned. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.
