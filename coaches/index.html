The bar

Coaches don't list themselves here.

They apply, they're vetted against our review standards, and they stay only if their reviews hold that bar.

Who can coach here

You've been through the application you'd be reviewing, and you can show us where you landed. For college admissions that means an undergraduate program we can verify; for BS/MD, the double application itself. Past that: you write specifically, and you answer on time.

How we vet

We read every application ourselves. If it's a fit, we verify your program and your admissions history, then send you sample work to review, scored against the same standard student reviews are held to. That calibration step is the gate.

What every review has to do

Say something specific: what to cut, what's missing, what to do next. Land before the turnaround published on the review. Carry your name. Generic praise and line edits with no reasoning are how a review fails here, and a failed review gets re-reviewed at our cost, not the student's.

What you commit to

Nothing standing. You set your capacity one review at a time, and a week where you take none is a normal week. Routing is the only lever that moves: reviews go first to coaches whose work consistently holds the bar.

What we ask you not to do

Don't write the student's essay; coach it. Don't promise anyone an outcome. Don't take a family off-platform, because the consent, the protections, and the record of the student's file live here. If you have a personal stake in an applicant, flag it and we'll route the review elsewhere.

FAQ

Questions coaches ask.

What does Craneweave pay per review?

Published rates, per review: $45 for an essay review, $30 for a school list, $110 for a full application, $20 for a check-in, and $25 on top for a 24-hour rush. The rates are the same for every coach on the platform, and you see the rate before you accept.

How do you become a college admissions coach here?

You apply with what you've actually done: your school or program, your admissions history, and your background. We read every application, verify it, and score a sample review against our review standards. Once you're on, reviews arrive in a queue and you accept only the ones that fit your expertise. No calendars, no unpaid intro calls.

How much will I make?

We publish what each review pays and nothing beyond that, because you decide how many you accept. Anyone quoting you a monthly figure for work like this is guessing at your calendar. The rates are above; the arithmetic is yours.

Is there a minimum, or a schedule?

Neither. There's no shift, no quota, and no consequence for a quiet week. Reviews sit in your queue with a deadline attached and you take the ones you want. If you take none, the queue simply routes elsewhere until you're back.

Do I have to talk to students or parents?

The work is written. A coordinator handles the relationship, the reminders, and anything administrative, so your side of it is the review itself. You are never asked to run an unpaid intro call or sell anything to a family.

What does the platform draft, and what's left for me?

The platform assembles the file and drafts the groundwork: what changed since the last draft, which prompt this answers, what the plan says comes next. None of it goes out unread. The judgment — what's working, what to cut, what's missing, what to do about it — is yours, and it's what your name is attached to. Everything that isn't judgment — matching, plans, reminders, deadlines, billing, support — belongs to the platform. That's why a family pays $299 instead of $10,000.

Which goals can I coach?

College admissions and BS/MD are enrolling students now, so that's where the reviews are today. MBA, law school, medical school, and banking and consulting recruiting open next as founding cohorts. Tell us on the application which of them you've actually been through — we route by experience, not by interest.

Am I expected to guarantee results?

Never. Admission isn't a purchasable outcome and we don't sell one — walk away from any platform that asks you to imply otherwise. What we promise families is the work: every essay, list, and application read by a named coach before it ships, on schedule. That's the only promise you're asked to keep.

What happens to a student's work and data?

Parents consent before any student under 18 joins. A student's work is used only to coach that student, deleted on request, and never sold — which also means it's not yours to keep, reuse, or share once the review is returned. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.
