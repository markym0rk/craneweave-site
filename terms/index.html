Program terms.

Last updated: August 2026

The deposit

Reserving a spot costs a $50 deposit. It is fully refundable any time before your onboarding begins — email team@craneweave.com and we refund it, no questions asked. When your program starts, the deposit credits in full toward your first month.

The program

Craneweave is a 12-month admissions coaching program billed monthly at the price shown when you reserved. Each month includes six expert reviews and the program entitlements listed on the pricing page. Unused reviews don’t roll over; the pace of the plan is designed so you won’t need them to.

Billing and cancellation

Billing is monthly through Stripe. You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current billing month — no cancellation fees, no lock-in. The program is built as a 12-month arc, and it works best run start to finish, but you are never trapped in it.

What we promise — and what we don’t

We promise that every essay, school list, and application submitted through the program is reviewed and approved by a named coach before feedback reaches your student, on the schedule shown in your plan.

We do not guarantee admission to any school or program, and you should be skeptical of anyone who does. Admissions outcomes depend on factors no coach controls.

Academic integrity

We coach; we don’t ghostwrite. All submitted work must be the student’s own. Our AI tools organize, check, and prompt — they do not write application essays, and our coaches will flag work that doesn’t sound like the student who submitted it. That standard protects your student: admissions offices are better at detecting outsourced essays than most families expect.

Coaching relationship

Craneweave provides educational coaching and application strategy. We are not a school, a placement agency, or agents of any university.
