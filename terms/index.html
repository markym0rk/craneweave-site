Terms of service

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

Monthly plans you can cancel any time. Every price is published. Every piece of work you send gets an expert written review on the published turnaround — nothing goes out unchecked. No guarantees of admission — from us or anyone honest.

The program

Craneweave is a coaching subscription — monthly plans you can cancel at any time, a dated Season Pass, or single à la carte reviews. There is no contract and no minimum term. Each plan includes the number of review credits published for that plan, returned on the published turnaround. A review is a scoped, written expert evaluation of one piece of real work — an essay draft, a school list, a full application, or a check-in — from your coach, who is named on every review.

Plans and billing

Every price is published at craneweave.com/pricing and is the price you pay.

  • Core — $299/month. 4 review credits each billing month, 72-hour review turnaround.
  • Plus — $499/month. 8 review credits each billing month, 48-hour turnaround, priority coach matching.
  • Season Pass — $1,999. Aug 1 – Jan 15, 24 review credits, priority access in the November crunch.
  • À la carte reviews — essay review $99 · school list $69 · full application $249 · check-in $49 · 24-hour rush +$50.

Signing up

You choose a plan at its published price and pay the first month through our payment processor. There is no deposit and no reservation fee, and there is never a sales call.

Review credits

Monthly review credits are issued each billing month and expire at the end of it. Season Pass credits are issued once and may be used any time between Aug 1 and Jan 15. If a review misses its published turnaround, it’s free.

Billing and cancellation

Billing is monthly through our payment processor. There is no minimum term and no contract. You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current billing month — no cancellation fees, no lock-in. Every plan bills at the published plan prices, and how student data is handled is set out in the privacy policy.

What we promise — and what we don’t

We promise the work: every essay, list, and application you send gets an expert written review on the published turnaround, from a coach who is named on every review — nothing goes out unchecked. We do not promise admission, a scholarship, or an offer — nobody can. Plans bill monthly at the published price and you can cancel monthly.

We do not guarantee admission to any school or program, and you should be skeptical of anyone who does. Admissions outcomes depend on factors no coach controls.

Academic integrity

We coach; we don’t ghostwrite. All submitted work must be the student’s own. The platform organizes, checks, and prompts — it does not write application essays, and our coaches will flag work that doesn’t sound like the student who submitted it.

Coaching relationship

Craneweave provides educational coaching and application strategy. We are not a school, a placement agency, or agents of any university.
