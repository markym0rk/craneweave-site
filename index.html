Questions families ask.

What is Craneweave?

Craneweave is a coaching platform for admissions and recruiting. You match with a vetted coach, get a personalized week-by-week plan, and send real work — essays, school lists, full applications — for written review on a published turnaround. Plans start at $299 a month, published, with no sales call.

Who reviews my student's work?

Your student's work is reviewed by a vetted coach who has been through the process they're facing — matched to your goal, named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards. You can re-match any time, or spend one credit on a second opinion from another vetted coach.

What does the platform do, and what does the coach do?

The platform runs the structure: the week-by-week plan, the modules that make each draft review-ready, deadline checks, reminders, and logistics. Your coach does what only an expert can — judges the essay, calibrates the school list, decides the strategy. Every judgment call carries a coach's name.

What exactly is a review?

A review is a scoped, written expert evaluation of one piece of real work — an essay draft, a school list, a full application, or a check-in — returned on a published turnaround and signed by your coach by name. Everything here is priced in review credits, which is why the pricing can be this simple.

How much does admissions coaching cost?

Admissions coaching on Craneweave costs $299 a month on Core — four review credits, 72-hour turnaround. Plus is $499 for eight credits and 48 hours. Season Pass is $1,999 for the senior season, August 1 to January 15. À la carte reviews start at $49. Cancel monthly, no contracts.

What if we want calls?

We don't sell calls: Craneweave is written, not scheduled — reviews you can reread the night before a deadline, plus a coordinator you can message any time. Everything your coach gives you comes in writing with their name on it — most of why this costs $299, not $10,000.

Do you guarantee admission?

No, we don't guarantee admission — and you should walk away from anyone who does. Admissions is not a purchasable outcome. What we promise is the work: every essay, list, and application reviewed by a named coach before it ships, on schedule.

My goal says "founding cohort." What does that mean?

"Founding cohort" means your goal opens next: college admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now; MBA, law, medical school, and recruiting follow, at the same published pricing. Joining the list costs nothing and holds your place — we'll email you the moment your goal opens.

What happens to my student's essays and data?

Your student's essays and data are used only to coach that student, and never sold. Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Work is kept while we're working together and deleted within 90 days after — sooner on request. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.
