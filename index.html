The honest part

Yes, we use AI. Here’s exactly how.

AI does

  • Drafts — brainstorm prompts, outlines, checklists
  • Checks — grammar, word counts, requirements, deadlines
  • Organizes — every task, file, and school in one plan
  • Reminds — so nothing slips while everyone’s busy

Your coach does

  • Judges — whether an essay actually works
  • Decides — the school list, the strategy, the trade-offs
  • Approves — everything, before it reaches you
  • Knows you — your file, your voice, your goals

Nothing reaches you without your coach’s sign-off. That’s not a disclaimer — it’s why this costs $299 and not $10,000.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Is AI doing the coaching?

No. AI drafts, checks, organizes, and reminds — the busywork. Every judgment call, whether an essay works, which schools make the list, what to fix next, is made by Michael or Mark. Nothing reaches your student without a founder’s sign-off, and the verification chip on every returned piece of work tells you exactly who approved it.

Who exactly reviews my student’s work?

The two people above, by name: Michael Lai and Mark Kim. We don’t route work to a network of contractors. It’s also why seats are limited — two coaches, fixed capacity, one cohort.

What if we want calls?

The program is async-first: written reviews, on a schedule, that you can reread the night before a deadline. That’s deliberate — written review is how two coaches hold quality across every student, and it’s most of why this costs $299 and not $10,000. Milestones like school-list calibration are live conversations; day-to-day feedback is written.

Do you guarantee admission?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who does. What we do guarantee: every essay, list, and application reviewed by a named founder before it ships, on schedule, with feedback specific enough to act on. If we’re not the right fit, your deposit comes back in full.

What happens to my student’s essays and data?

Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Student work is used only to coach that student, retained only as long as we work together (deleted on request), and never used to train third-party AI models. The full policy is at craneweave.com/privacy.

When does the cohort start?

Onboarding for the Fall 2026 cohort begins in September. Your deposit reserves a seat; we confirm your onboarding window by email within a few days.

What’s the refund policy?

The $50 deposit is fully refundable any time before onboarding, no questions asked. When the program starts, it credits to your first month. Full terms at craneweave.com/terms.
