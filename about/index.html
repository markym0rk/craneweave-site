The standard

  • Every review carries a name.

    Clear, human feedback from the coach who reviewed your work.

  • Carefully selected coaches.

    Every coach brings relevant firsthand experience and completes an evaluated sample review before working with clients.

  • Honest promises.

    We cannot guarantee an admission, offer, or performance outcome. We promise thoughtful expert review, clear next steps, and feedback on schedule.

  • Your work stays yours.

    Used only to coach you. Never sold or used to train AI models. Deleted within 90 days after coaching or a program ends—sooner if you ask.

  • Clear pricing.

    Straightforward individual plans, complete written scopes for teams and cohorts, and no hidden fees or surprises.
