Questions about Craneweave.

How are Craneweave coaches vetted?

Craneweave coaches are vetted, not listed — nobody posts a profile here. Coaches apply with what they've actually done, and we vet them against our review standards before they read a student's work. Every review goes out signed by name, and coaches stay only while their reviews hold the bar. Routing favors consistently strong reviewers.

Who founded Craneweave?

Craneweave was founded by Mark Kim and Michael Lai, two coaches who ran these applications themselves. Mark went to Princeton and coached applicants independently, one family at a time. Michael went through Hofstra's BS/MD program, ran the double application — a college application and a medical-school application at once, on two calendars for two audiences — and coached BS/MD applicants professionally.

Do the founders coach every student?

No — the founders don't coach every student. They are two of the coaches here, not the roster. They coach on the platform, held to the same standards and published turnarounds as anyone we bring on. Matching follows your goal, and the name on your review is whoever actually read your work — founder or not.

How much does Craneweave cost?

Craneweave costs $299 a month on Core — four review credits, 72-hour turnaround. Plus is $499 for eight credits and 48 hours. Season Pass is $1,999 for the senior season, August 1 to January 15. BS/MD runs from $399 a month. À la carte reviews start at $49 — the full list is on the pricing page. Cancel monthly, no contracts.

Why are the prices published?

The number on the site is the number — the same for every family who lands here. There is no quote to prepare, no discount to negotiate, and no upsell waiting at the deadline, when a family is most willing to pay anything.

Which goals are open now?

Two goals are open now: college admissions is enrolling, and the BS/MD track is live from $399 a month. MBA, law school, medical school, and banking and consulting recruiting are founding-cohort goals with a free waitlist. Every goal that opens after them opens at the same published prices.

What happens to a student's work?

A student's work is used only to coach that student — never sold. Parents consent before any student under 18 joins. Work is kept while we are working together and deleted within 90 days after that — sooner on request. The full policy is on the privacy page.
