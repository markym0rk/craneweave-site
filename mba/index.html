FAQ

Questions about the MBA cohort.

When does MBA admissions coaching open?

MBA opens as a founding cohort for Round 2 — January. It launches reviews-first: written, signed reads of your essays, your resume and short answers, your school list, and one full-application read before you submit. We won't print a date here we can't be certain of, so joining the list is how you find out first: you'll get an email the moment it opens, and nothing before then. Joining is free, holds your place, and there is no sales call. College admissions and BS/MD are enrolling today.

What does an MBA coach review?

A review is a scoped, written evaluation of one piece of real work, returned on a published turnaround and signed by your coach by name. MBA opens with four: your essays, your resume and short answers, your school list, and one full-application read before you submit. Resume and short answers count as one essay-review credit.

Who will review my work?

A vetted coach who has been through MBA admissions, matched to your programs and your round. Coaches don't list themselves here — they apply, they're vetted against our review standards, and they keep coaching here only if their reviews hold the bar. Every review comes back with a name on it, and you can request a re-match at any time.

What will MBA admissions coaching cost?

The same published prices as every other goal: $299 a month on Core for four review credits at a 72-hour turnaround, $499 on Plus for eight credits at 48 hours. À la carte, an essay review is $99, a school list $69, a full application $249, a check-in $49, and a 24-hour rush is $50 more. No surcharge for a harder goal. Cancel monthly, no contracts.

Does joining cost anything?

No. Holding your place is free — no deposit, no card, no commitment. When MBA opens you'll get an email with the same published prices as every other goal: $299 a month for four review credits, $499 for eight. If it isn't for you then, do nothing.

I'm applying in Round 1 next fall, not Round 2. Should I still join?

Yes. Round 2 is when we open, not the only round we coach. Put your timeline in the form and we'll email you when the cohort opens — starting a cycle early is the easier version of this, not the wasted one.

Do you guarantee I'll get in?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who tells you they can. Admission isn't a purchasable outcome. What we promise is the work: every essay and every application read by a named coach before you submit, on a published turnaround, specific enough to act on.
