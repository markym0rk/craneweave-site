FAQ

Questions BS/MD families ask.

What makes BS/MD different from regular admissions?

It's two applications in one season. You submit a full college application and, in effect, a medical school application — different essays, different readers, different standards — and many programs add interviews on top. The work doesn't just double; the two halves have to agree with each other. That's what this track is built for.

How is BS/MD admissions coaching different from regular college coaching?

A BS/MD applicant runs two applications in one season, so one coach carries both. That means a program list built from each program's own eligibility, curriculum, and testing rules; both essay tracks — the college personal statement and the why-medicine essay; clinical and research experience written so a committee can read it; interview prep when invitations land, program by program; and a final audit of the whole application before anything is submitted.

What does BS/MD admissions coaching cost?

The BS/MD track is $399 a month. That covers the vetted coach match, the week-by-week plan, guided modules from program list to final audit, written reviews on either essay track, and interview prep when invitations land. Cancel monthly, from any month, and request a different coach at any point. Nothing reaches you without a named coach's sign-off. À la carte reviews start at $49.

How do both essay tracks fit into one plan?

One calendar, two lanes. The personal statement is written for an admissions office; the why-medicine essay is written for a committee of physicians. Your coach sets the order, the plan gates each draft behind the module that makes it review-ready, and reviews alternate so neither track stalls the other.

Do you help with interviews?

Yes. When invitations land, your plan turns to interview prep: the formats each program uses, the questions committees actually push on, and specific written feedback on your answers. Like every other milestone here, it's written work read by your coach and returned on a published turnaround.

When should we start?

Junior year is the natural start — the program list comes together in the spring and both essay tracks run through the summer, which is the only stretch of the year with room in it. If you're already into senior fall, we'll tell you honestly what's still worth doing and what isn't.

Do you guarantee admission?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who does. Nobody can sell you a seat in a combined-degree program. What we promise is the work: every essay, list, and application reviewed by a named coach before it ships, on schedule. We also make sure the college half stands on its own, because for most applicants that's the half that has to land.

What happens to my student's essays and data?

Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Student work is used only to coach that student and never sold. It's kept while we're working together and deleted within 90 days after that — sooner on request. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.
