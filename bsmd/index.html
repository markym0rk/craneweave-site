Questions BS/MD families ask.

What makes BS/MD different from regular admissions?

BS/MD is different from regular admissions because it's two applications in one season: a full college application and, in effect, a medical school application — different essays, different readers, different standards — and many programs add interviews on top. The work doesn't just double; the two halves have to agree with each other.

How is BS/MD admissions coaching different from regular college coaching?

BS/MD admissions coaching differs from regular college coaching because one coach carries two applications in one season: a program list built from each program's own eligibility, curriculum, and testing rules; both essay tracks — personal statement and why-medicine; clinical and research experience written so a committee can read it; interview prep; and a final audit before submission.

What does BS/MD admissions coaching cost?

BS/MD admissions coaching costs $399 a month. That covers the vetted coach match, the week-by-week plan, guided modules from program list to final audit, written reviews on either essay track, and interview prep when invitations land. Cancel monthly; request a different coach any time. À la carte reviews start at $49.

How do both essay tracks fit into one plan?

Both essay tracks fit on one calendar, in two lanes. The personal statement is written for an admissions office; the why-medicine essay for a committee of physicians. Your coach sets the order, each draft is gated behind the module that makes it review-ready, and reviews alternate so neither track stalls.

Do you help with interviews?

Yes — interview prep is part of the plan. When invitations land, your plan turns to the formats each program uses, the questions committees actually push on, and specific written feedback on your answers. Like every other milestone, it's written work read by your coach and returned on a published turnaround.

When should we start?

Junior year is the natural start — the program list comes together in the spring and both essay tracks run through the summer, which is the only stretch of the year with room in it. If you're already into senior fall, we'll tell you honestly what's still worth doing and what isn't.

Do you guarantee admission?

No — we don't guarantee admission, and you should walk away from anyone who does. Nobody can sell you a seat in a combined-degree program. What we promise is the work: every essay, list, and application reviewed by a named coach before it ships, on schedule — and a college half that stands on its own.

What happens to my student's essays and data?

Your student's essays and data are used only to coach that student — never sold. Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Work is kept while we're working together and deleted within 90 days after that, sooner on request. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.
