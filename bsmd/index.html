FAQ

Questions BS/MD parents ask.

What makes BS/MD different from regular admissions?

It’s two applications in one season: a full college application and, effectively, a med-school application — different essays, different readers, different standards — often with interviews layered on top. The calendar is tighter, the admit rates are lower than the Ivies, and a mistake in either half sinks both. That’s why the track exists.

How many programs do you cover?

All of them. There are roughly fifty BS/MD and BA/MD programs in the country, and the track starts with a full sweep: eligibility, curriculum requirements, MCAT policies, and realistic odds for your student’s file. The shortlist comes out of that calibration with Michael — not from a listicle.

Do you prep for interviews?

Yes. When invitations land, prep moves live: traditional panels, MMI formats, ethics questions, and the “why medicine” follow-ups committees actually push on. Prep is program-specific, with the coach who sat in those chairs.

What does the platform actually do?

It holds the double application together: both essay tracks on one calendar, modules that get each piece of work review-ready before it’s submitted, requirement checks across every program’s quirks, and reminders through the hardest season. Every judgment call is made by Michael or Mark, and nothing reaches your student without a founder’s sign-off — the named approval on every returned piece of work tells you exactly who read it.

Do you guarantee admission?

No — at sub-5% admit rates, anyone who guarantees BS/MD admission is lying to you. What we guarantee: an honest program list, every piece of work reviewed by a named founder before it ships, and a parallel college application strong enough to stand on its own.

What happens to my student’s essays and data?

Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Student work is used only to coach that student, retained only as long as we work together (deleted on request), and never sold or shared beyond the processors that run the service. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.

What’s the refund policy?

The $50 deposit is fully refundable any time before onboarding, no questions asked. When the program starts, it credits to your first month. Full terms at craneweave.com/terms.
