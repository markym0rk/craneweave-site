FAQ

Questions before you join.

What does "founding cohort" mean?

Banking and consulting isn't open yet. College admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now; recruiting is one of the goals that opens next, alongside MBA admissions, also opening as a founding cohort. Joining the list is free, holds your place, and commits you to nothing — we'll email you the moment it opens, at the pricing published on this site. You sign up for yourself: no consult call, no parent required.

What can a coach review for recruiting?

Four kinds of work. Resume and cover letter, line by line: which bullets carry weight, which read as filler, and which one is actually your strongest and buried at the bottom — rewritten with you, not for you. Your story bank: the handful of stories you'll retell all cycle, pressure-tested for specifics and structure, including the "why banking" and "why consulting" answers that usually come out rehearsed. Case and technical reps: you record the rep against a real prompt and send it in, and a coach who has run these from the other side of the table returns the debrief in writing so you can act on it before the next one. Firm list and applications: which firms, which programs, which dates — calibrated to where you actually are — and a read on the full application before you send it rather than after.

What does a case interview coach do here?

Reps, in writing, from someone who has worked the seat. You send a case or technical rep, a resume, or a behavioral story, and one scoped review comes back signed. Your coach sets a week-by-week plan you can see — not a folder of templates — with each review scoped to one thing and reps that get harder as the rounds get closer. What a rep costs is published before the cohort opens; like every other number on this site, you won't have to call to find it out.

Do you coach investment banking interviews too?

Yes. This page covers both tracks. Consulting recruiting runs on case reps; banking recruiting runs on technical reps and the story bank. Either way you're matched to a coach who has worked the seat you're recruiting for, and each review is scoped to one thing.

Who would be reviewing my work?

A vetted coach who has worked the job you're recruiting for, matched to your goal and named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards; they don't simply list themselves here. You can request a re-match at any time, and you can ask for a second opinion — another vetted coach’s read on the same work — for one credit, like any other review.

Can you get me a referral or an interview?

No. We don't broker introductions, and you should be skeptical of anyone who says they will. What we do is make the work you send in as strong as it can be — the resume, the cover letter, the stories, the reps — so the introductions you make yourself land better.

Is this a course or a template pack?

No. There are guided modules that get each piece ready for review, but what you're paying for is an expert reading your actual resume, your actual stories, and your actual case reps, then signing the feedback. A review is a scoped, written evaluation of one piece of real work, returned on a published turnaround.

Do you guarantee an offer?

No — and walk away from anyone who promises one. Recruiting outcomes are not purchasable. What we promise is the work: everything you send reviewed by a named coach, on schedule, specific enough to act on before the next round.
