Questions before you join.

What does "founding cohort" mean?

A founding cohort means banking and consulting isn't open yet — college admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now, and recruiting follows as a founding cohort alongside MBA admissions. Joining the list is free, holds your place, and commits you to nothing; we'll email you when it opens, at the pricing on this page. You sign up for yourself — no sales call, no parent required.

What can a coach review for recruiting?

For recruiting, a coach can review four kinds of work: your resume and cover letter, line by line, rewritten with you, not for you; your story bank, including the "why banking" and "why consulting" answers; case and technical reps you record and send in, debriefed in writing; and your firm list and full applications, read before you submit.

What does a case interview coach do here?

A case interview coach here does reps, in writing: you send a case or technical rep, a resume, or a behavioral story, and one scoped review comes back signed by name. Your coach sets a week-by-week plan you can see, with reps that get harder as rounds get closer — at prices published before the cohort opens.

Do you coach investment banking interviews too?

Yes — we coach investment banking interviews as well as consulting. Consulting recruiting runs on case reps; banking recruiting runs on technical reps and the story bank. Either way you're matched to a coach who has worked the seat you're recruiting for, and each review is scoped to one thing.

Who would be reviewing my work?

Your work is reviewed by a vetted coach who has worked the job you're recruiting for — matched to your goal, named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards; they don't list themselves here. You can re-match at any time, or spend one credit on a second coach's read of the same work.

Can you get me a referral or an interview?

No — we can't get you a referral or an interview, and be skeptical of anyone who says they can. What we do is make the work you send as strong as it can be — resume, cover letter, stories, reps — so the introductions you make yourself land better.

Is this a course or a template pack?

No — this isn't a course or a template pack. Guided modules get each piece ready for review, but you're paying for an expert reading your actual resume, stories, and case reps, then signing the feedback. A review is a scoped, written evaluation of one piece of real work, returned on a published turnaround.

Do you guarantee an offer?

No — we don't guarantee an offer, and walk away from anyone who promises one. Recruiting outcomes are not purchasable. What we promise is the work: everything you send reviewed by a named coach, on schedule, specific enough to act on before the next round.
