What a review buys

One review = one piece of real work — an essay draft, a school list, a full application, a workflow — evaluated in writing by your coach, returned on the published turnaround, with clear direction on what to change and why.

Reviews reset monthly (Core and Plus) or pool for the season (Season Pass). Spend one on a second opinion from a different coach any time. Unused reviews don't roll over.

Why this costs $299 and not $10,000

A $10,000 consultant charges expert rates for everything — including deadline reminders and questions a module answers better. We built software to do everything that isn't expert judgment: the plan, the prep, the tracking, the logistics. What's left is the only thing worth expert prices — an expert, reviewing your real work. You pay for exactly that.
