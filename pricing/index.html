What an included review credit covers

Core, Plus, and the Five-Month Plan include review credits. Each credit covers one focused piece of real work—such as an essay draft, school list, résumé, interview response, AI workflow, or final application—evaluated in writing by your coach, with clear direction on what to change and why.

Standard individual plans are built around written review. Recurring calls are not included unless they are stated separately in writing.

Core and Plus credits expire at the end of each billing month. Five-Month Plan credits can be used at any point during the five-month plan. Your coach is backed by the broader Craneweave network, so you can spend a review credit for a second expert perspective at any time.
