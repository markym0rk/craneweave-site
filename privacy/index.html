Privacy policy.

Last updated: August 2026

Craneweave is an admissions coaching program. Most of our students are under 18, so this policy is written for the person who actually reads it: a parent deciding whether to trust us with their student’s work.

Parental consent

A parent or legal guardian creates the account, agrees to these terms, and consents before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Parents can see their student’s plan and submitted work, and can withdraw consent — and the account — at any time by emailing team@craneweave.com.

What we collect

  • Account information — parent name, email, student name and graduation year.
  • Student work — essays, drafts, school lists, activity descriptions, and anything else submitted for review.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Usage analytics — how the site and program are used, including which price you were shown, so we can run the program honestly and improve it.

How we use it

Student work is used for one purpose: coaching that student. Our coaches read it, the platform helps organize and check it, and feedback comes back with a named coach’s sign-off.

We never sell personal data, and we never allow student work to be used to train third-party AI models — full stop. Any tooling that processes student work does so under agreements that prohibit use beyond coaching that student.

Retention and deletion

We keep student work for as long as the student is in the program, and delete it within 90 days after the program ends — sooner on request. Email us and we will delete a student’s work and personal data, confirm in writing, and keep only what payment regulations require us to retain.

Who we share with

Only the processors needed to run the service: Stripe (payments), our hosting provider, and our analytics tooling. Each receives the minimum necessary and none may use the data for their own purposes.

Your rights

Parents can request a copy of their student’s data, correct it, or delete it at any time. Write to team@craneweave.com and we’ll respond within a week.
