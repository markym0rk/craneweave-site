Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version, for parents

Your student’s essays and data are used for one thing: coaching your student. Never sold, never used to train AI models. Parents consent before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Everything is deleted within 90 days after we finish working together — sooner on request. The full legal version is below.

Craneweave is a coaching platform for college, BS/MD, MBA, law, medical school, and recruiting applicants, and for teams and cohorts. Many of our students are under 18, so this policy leads with parental consent — the sections below apply to every account. For organization pilots and cohort programs, the same rules apply to every participant’s submitted work, and access is limited to the assigned coach and coordinator.

Applicants who are 18 or older hold their own accounts and consent for themselves. No parent is required.

What happens to my student’s essays?

Student work is used only to coach that student, and it is never sold. It is kept while we’re working together and deleted within 90 days after that — sooner if you ask. A parent or guardian consents before any student under 18 uses Craneweave, and either of you can ask us to stop.

Parental consent

A parent or legal guardian creates the account, agrees to these terms, and consents before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Parents can see their student’s plan and submitted work, and can withdraw consent — and the account — at any time by emailing team@craneweave.com.

What we collect

  • Account information — account holder name and email, student name, and graduation year.
  • Student work — essays, drafts, school lists, activity descriptions, and anything else submitted for review.
  • Payment information — processed by our payment processor. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Usage analytics — how the site and program are used, so we can improve them.

How we use it

Student work is used for one purpose: coaching that student. Our coaches read it, the platform helps organize and check it, and feedback comes back from a named coach.

We never sell personal data, and we never use submitted work to train AI models — ours or anyone else’s. Any tooling that processes submitted work does so under agreements that prohibit use beyond coaching that person.

Retention and deletion

We keep student work for as long as the student is in the program, and delete it within 90 days after the program ends — sooner on request. Email us and we will delete a student’s work and personal data, confirm in writing, and keep only what payment regulations require us to retain.

Who we share with

Only the processors needed to run the service: our payment processor, our hosting provider, and our analytics tooling. Each receives the minimum necessary and none may use the data for their own purposes.

Your rights

Parents — or the applicant, when they are 18 or over — can request a copy of the account’s data, correct it, or delete it at any time. Email the team and we’ll respond within a week. This policy sits alongside the program terms and what we promise and what we don’t.
