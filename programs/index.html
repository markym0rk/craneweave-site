Every student in your cohort gets a coach.

Access organizations, districts, and scholarship programs run Craneweave as a cohort — personalized coaching for every student, with the reporting funders ask for.

Request a cohort quote in writing

How a cohort runs

Enroll your students in one intake. Each is matched to an experienced coach and a step-by-step plan for their goal. A coordinator handles logistics; your staff sees progress, not paperwork.

The reporting funders ask for

Per-cohort and per-student: module completion, review usage, on-pace-to-deadline. Exportable, mid-program and final. Real numbers from real work.

Cohort pricing

Quoted in writing off the published plans — the same public rates families pay, at cohort scale.

Program FAQ

  • Consent and minors: parents/guardians consent before any student under 18 is enrolled.

  • Data: student work is used only to coach that student, never sold, deleted within 90 days after the program.

  • What your counselors see: progress and pacing — never student essays without student consent.
