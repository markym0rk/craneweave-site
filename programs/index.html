FAQ

Questions programs ask.

How does a Craneweave cohort work for a district or program?

You tell us the cohort size and the goals. We quote a cohort rate off the published plans, in writing, before any call. Every student is then matched with a vetted coach and a personalized plan, and you get module completion, review utilization, and on-pace reporting your funders ask for. The platform carries the structure, so coaching doesn't get thinner as the cohort gets bigger.

Why do programs need this if students have school counselors?

The national average is 372 students per public school counselor (ASCA). At that ratio, an expert read of every student's essays isn't possible, however good the counselor is. The private consultants who fill that gap cost $10,000 and up for a single cycle. A cohort adds one vetted coach per student, on a personalized plan, so the students who need the most attention still get it.

How does cohort pricing work?

Every seat is one of the published plans, and cohorts are quoted at a volume rate off those prices. Tell us the seat count, the goals, and the term, and you'll have the number in an email — before any call. If you want to talk after that, we'll talk.

Who coaches our students?

Vetted coaches matched to each student's goal, named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards; they don't simply list themselves. Where your program has alumni who meet that bar, we recruit them first — they're paid per review like every other coach. The coach page has the rates, the standards, and the application for alumni who want to coach your students.

What reporting do we get?

Module completion, review utilization, and who is on pace for their deadlines — per student and per cohort, current rather than end-of-term, and ready to export when the grant report comes due. It's built for the question a funder actually asks: did the students you paid for get the coaching, and did they use it?

Most of our students are minors. How do consent and data work?

A parent or guardian consents before any student under 18 joins. Student work is used only to coach that student and never sold. It's kept while we're working together and deleted within 90 days after that — sooner on request. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.

Which goals can a cohort use today?

College admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now. MBA, law, medical school, and banking & consulting recruiting open next in founding cohorts, with the same published pricing. If your cohort spans several goals, say so in your note and we'll tell you what's live for each.

Do you promise outcomes?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who promises your students results in this process. What we report is the work: what was assigned, what was submitted, what came back reviewed and signed, and whether it happened on time.
