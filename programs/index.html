Questions programs ask.

How does Craneweave run college counseling for a district or program?

Craneweave runs college counseling for a district or program as a cohort. You send the cohort size and goals; a cohort rate off the published plans comes back in writing before any call. Every student is matched with a vetted coach and a plan, and you get completion, utilization, and on-pace reporting. Coaching doesn't get thinner as the cohort grows.

Why do programs need this if students have school counselors?

Programs need this because school counselors carry an average of 372 students each (ASCA) — at that ratio, an expert read of every student's essays isn't possible. The private consultants who fill that gap start at $10,000 a cycle. A cohort adds one vetted coach per student, so the students who need the most attention get it.

How does cohort pricing work?

Cohort pricing starts from the published plans: every seat is one of them, and cohorts are quoted at a volume rate off those prices. Tell us the seat count, the goals, and the term, and you'll have the number in an email — before any call. If you want to talk after that, we'll talk.

Who coaches our students?

Your students are coached by vetted coaches matched to their goal and named on every review. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards; nobody lists themselves. Where your program has alumni who meet that bar, we recruit them first, paid per review like every other coach — rates and application on the coach page.

What reporting do we get?

The reporting you get is module completion, review utilization, and who is on pace for their deadlines — per student and per cohort, current rather than end-of-term, ready to export when the grant report comes due. It's built for the question a funder asks: did the students you paid for get the coaching, and did they use it?

Most of our students are minors. How do consent and data work?

A parent or guardian consents before any student under 18 joins. Student work is used only to coach that student and never sold. It's kept while we're working together and deleted within 90 days after that — sooner on request. Full policy at craneweave.com/privacy.

Which goals can a cohort use today?

Today a cohort can use college admissions and BS/MD, both enrolling now. MBA, law, medical school, and banking & consulting recruiting open next in founding cohorts, at the same published pricing. If your cohort spans several goals, say so in your note and we'll tell you what's live for each.

Do you promise outcomes?

No — we don't promise outcomes, and you should walk away from anyone who does promise your students results in this process. What we report is the work: what was assigned, what was submitted, what came back reviewed and signed, and whether it happened on time.
