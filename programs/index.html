What students and programs receive

Personalized for each student. Manageable for your team.

Each student receives

  • A named coach

    Matched to the student’s goal and stage.

  • A goal-specific plan

    Milestones, deadlines, and the next useful step in one place.

  • Review of real work

    Clear written feedback on essays, lists, applications, résumés, or interview practice.

Your program receives

  • One coordinated intake

    Enroll the cohort without matching students by hand.

  • A Craneweave program coordinator

    One point of contact for setup, pacing, and questions.

  • Progress you can act on

    Participation, review usage, and whether students are on pace for key deadlines.

  • A shareable summary

    Cohort-level reporting for program leaders and funders.

How a cohort runs

Enroll once. Support each student individually.

01

Scope

Choose the students, goals, timing, and level of support. We confirm the program and price in writing before anything begins.

02

Match

Each student completes one intake and is matched with a coach whose experience fits the goal.

03

Coach

Students follow their own plan and submit real work for written feedback. A Craneweave coordinator keeps participation and pacing visible.

04

Report

Your team receives cohort-level progress throughout the program and a final summary when it ends.

A clear scope before you commit.

Trust and privacy

Student work stays student work.

  • A parent or guardian consents before any student under 18 is enrolled.

  • Submitted work is used only to coach that student and is never sold or used to train AI models.

  • Counselors and program leaders see participation and pacing—not a student’s submitted work without that student’s consent.

  • Submitted work is deleted within 90 days after the program ends, sooner on request.
