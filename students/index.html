A review is one piece of real work.

It's the unit everything here is priced in, returned on a published turnaround.

Essay review

One full draft — personal statement, supplement, or activities description. A structural read of whether the essay works, line notes where the writing gets in the way, and an ordered list of what to change next.

School list

Your list read against your student's record and your family's constraints — cost, distance, program, timing. What to add, what to cut, and the reasoning behind each call, so reach, target, and likely are labels you can defend.

Full application

Everything before you submit — essays, activities, honors, the short answers filled in at midnight — read the way a reader on the other side would. A section-by-section verdict: ready to ship, one more pass, or quietly costing you.

Check-in

A written answer to one scoped question. Should we apply early? Is this activity worth the space? Is the list too top-heavy? Small, specific, and in writing, so you can reread it the night you decide.

Before you start.

What happens right after we sign up?

Right after you sign up, you tell us the goal, the timeline, and who's signing up. We match you with a coach vetted for that goal, your coach sets the first week-by-week plan, and the modules open. No call to schedule, no consultation fee, anywhere in that sequence.

How does college admissions coaching work?

College admissions coaching on Craneweave works in three steps. You tell us your goal and timeline, and we match you with a vetted coach. Your coach builds a week-by-week plan and assigns guided modules. You send real work — a draft, a school list, a finished application — and written feedback comes back on a published turnaround, signed by name.

What does a college essay review include?

A college essay review includes one scoped, written evaluation of one full draft. Your coach reads the actual draft, says which paragraph is the real essay, what to cut, and what to show instead — then reads the next draft. It comes back in 72 hours on Core, 48 on Plus.

How do review credits work?

Review credits work the same on every plan: four a month on Core, eight on Plus, 24 across the Season Pass. Spend them in any combination — essay review, school list, full application, check-in. Monthly credits reset each billing month, and single reviews are available à la carte at the published prices.

Can I message my coach between reviews?

Yes — message any time, and it goes to your coaching team: a coordinator keeps everything moving, and your coach's judgment arrives in the reviews. A review is the scoped, written, signed piece of work; messaging is how you ask the small questions in between. Messaging doesn't spend a credit; reviews do.

What am I actually paying for?

What you're actually paying for is the part only a person can do: judgment about your student's work, in writing, with a name on it. The structure — plans, modules, reminders, deadline math — is the platform's job. You never book a call to start, and nobody calls to upsell at the end.

Can we change coaches?

Yes — you can change coaches any time, and you don't owe us a reason. Ask for a re-match and we'll route you to another vetted coach. You can also ask for a second opinion — another coach's read on the same work, spending one credit like any other review.

Who should sign up — the parent or the student?

Either the parent or the student can sign up. Most families keep a parent on the account with the student doing the work, and a parent or guardian consents before any student under 18 uses Craneweave. Both of you can message the coaching team.

What if we use all our credits in a month?

If you use all your credits, buy a single review à la carte at the published price, or move up to Plus for eight a month. Running out pauses reviews, not coaching — your plan, modules, and coaching team stay the same. Unused credits don't roll over; your coach will say when to spend the last one.

Is it too late if we're starting senior fall?

No — starting senior fall is not too late. It's what the Season Pass is built for: Aug 1 to Jan 15, 24 credits, and priority access in the November crunch. Your coach builds the plan backward from your first deadline rather than forward from today.

Is admissions coaching worth it if we already have a school counselor?

Coaching is worth it alongside a school counselor because it adds a specialist with time to read the actual draft. A public school counselor carries about 372 students (ASCA) — a caseload that makes a line-by-line read of one essay close to impossible. That read is what a Craneweave review is. Keep both.

How do we cancel?

You cancel monthly, without a call — there are no contracts, no cancellation fees, and nobody phones to talk you out of it. À la carte reviews are one-time purchases, so there's nothing to cancel there at all.

Do you coach goals other than college?

Yes — we coach goals beyond college. College admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now. MBA, law school, medical school, and banking and consulting recruiting open next as founding cohorts, at the same published pricing. Joining a cohort list is free and holds your place.
