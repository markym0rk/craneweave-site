The longest application season there is — planned so nothing slips.

Primaries. Thirty secondaries in six weeks. Interviews into spring. Medical school admissions isn't one application, it's a nine-month campaign — and the applicants who make it through aren't the ones with the best first draft. They're the ones who never fell behind. That's what the plan is for. Then a coach who got in reviews everything that matters before you submit.

What gets reviewed

The personal statement. Work & activities descriptions — 15 chances to make your case, treated like it. Secondary essays, prioritized: you can't perfect thirty, so your coach tells you which schools earn the hours. Interview prep in writing — your answers, reviewed before you say them out loud.

The season map

Spring: statement + primaries. June: submit early — it matters. July–August: the secondary flood, triaged by the plan. Fall–winter: interviews. The plan tracks every school's status so you're never running your applications from a spreadsheet at 1am.
