FAQ

Questions applicants ask.

Do I sign up, or does a parent?

You do. Medical school applicants are adults, so you are the applicant and the account holder — you sign up for yourself, and every review comes back to you, signed by the coach who read it.

When does medical school open?

It opens for the 2027 cycle. We haven't published a date because we won't publish one we aren't sure we can hold — college admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now, and medical school is next in line. Joining the founding cohort is free, holds your place, and is the fastest way to hear the day matching opens.

What does a medical school application review include?

Personal comments, read draft by draft. Every work-and-activities entry, including the ones you mark most meaningful. A school list calibrated against your numbers, your state, and each school's mission. Secondaries, batched and turned around while the prompts are still landing. One read of the full application as a whole before you submit it. And interview prep — your answers to the questions that school actually asks, marked up before you sit down. Each of those is one review, and one credit.

How does the medical school cycle run?

Spring is for the school list, the activities, and the personal comments draft, all written while nothing is due yet — your test date and your committee letter set the earliest week you can realistically submit, so the plan starts there and works backwards. AMCAS opens in the spring and submission follows a few weeks after; verification takes weeks and everything downstream waits on it. Summer is secondary season: prompts arrive within days of verification, from every school on your list, more or less at once. Interviews run from late summer into winter, and schools can begin extending acceptances partway through the fall. Update letters and waitlist strategy carry into spring. Craneweave opens for the 2027 cycle.

Can you keep up with secondary season?

That is what the turnarounds and the credits are for. Core returns a review in 72 hours, Plus in 48, and a 24-hour rush is $50 when a deadline is close. The plan also front-loads the prompts that repeat across schools, so most secondaries start from something you have already written and had reviewed, not from a blank page at 1 a.m.

What will it cost when it opens?

The same published prices as every other goal on Craneweave: $299 a month for four review credits on a 72-hour turnaround, or $499 for eight credits on 48 hours with priority matching. À la carte, an essay review is $99, a school list is $69, a full application read is $249, a check-in is $49, and a 24-hour rush is $50 on top. Cancel monthly. No contracts.

Who would read my application?

A vetted coach matched to this goal — someone who has been through this application, not an anonymous queue. Coaches apply and are vetted against our review standards; they don't simply list themselves here. Every review is signed by name, and you can request a different match at any time, or ask for a second opinion — another vetted coach’s read on the same work — for one credit, like any other review. Your work is used only to coach you, deleted on request, and never sold — full policy here.

Do you guarantee an acceptance?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who does. Admission is not a purchasable outcome. What we commit to is the work: every essay, list, and application read by a named coach before it ships, on a turnaround we published in advance.
