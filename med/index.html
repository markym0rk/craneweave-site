Questions applicants ask.

Do I sign up, or does a parent?

You sign up, not a parent. Medical school applicants are adults, so you are the applicant and the account holder: you sign up for yourself, the plan is built with you, and every review comes back to you, signed by the coach who read it.

When does medical school open?

Medical school opens on Craneweave for the 2027 cycle. We haven't published a date because we won't publish one we aren't sure we can hold — college admissions and BS/MD are enrolling now; medical school follows for the 2027 cycle. Joining the founding cohort is free, holds your place, and gets you an email the day matching opens.

What does a medical school application review include?

A medical school application review covers one real piece of work: personal comments read draft by draft, work-and-activities entries, a school list calibrated to your numbers and your state, batched secondaries, one read of the full application before you submit, or interview prep against that school's own questions. Each is one review, and one credit.

How does the medical school admissions cycle run?

The medical school admissions cycle runs on one calendar. Spring: school list, activities, personal comments — before anything is due. AMCAS opens in spring; submission follows within weeks, then verification. Summer: secondaries from every school, almost at once. Interviews run late summer into winter; acceptances start in fall. Update letters and waitlists carry into spring. Craneweave opens for the 2027 cycle.

Can you keep up with secondary season?

Keeping up with secondary season is what the turnarounds and credits are for. Core returns a review in 72 hours, Plus in 48, and a 24-hour rush is $50 when a deadline is close. The plan front-loads prompts that repeat across schools, so most secondaries start from work you've already had reviewed — not a blank page at 1 a.m.

What will it cost when it opens?

It will cost the same published prices as every goal on Craneweave: $299 a month for four review credits on a 72-hour turnaround, or $499 for eight credits on 48 hours with priority matching. À la carte: essay review $99, school list $69, full application $249, check-in $49, 24-hour rush +$50. Cancel monthly. No contracts.

Who would read my application?

A vetted coach matched to this goal reads your application — someone who has been through it, not an anonymous queue. Coaches are vetted against our review standards; nobody lists themselves. Every review is signed by name; you can change your match anytime or ask for a second opinion for one credit. Your work is used only to coach you, deleted within 90 days — sooner on request, never sold — full policy here.

Do you guarantee an acceptance?

No — we don't guarantee an acceptance, and you should walk away from anyone who does. Admission is not a purchasable outcome. What we commit to is the work: every essay, list, and application read by a named coach before it ships, on a turnaround we published in advance.
